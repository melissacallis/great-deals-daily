## Identity
- **What it is (one sentence):** A daily Amazon deals site that lists products marked down from their usual price, plus hand-picked gift guides, so shoppers don't have to dig.
- **Category:** Deals & product-recommendation publisher (Amazon Associates affiliate).
- **Founded / location:** 2026, US (amazon.com marketplace).

## Positioning
- **What's different:** Real discounts only (items priced below their usual/list price), refreshed daily, plus a human-picked shortlist with a plain reason for each pick.
- **Why believe it:** Every deal shows the list price and the savings; picks explain *why* in one or two sentences; affiliate relationship is always disclosed.
- **Point of view:**
  - A deal is only a deal if the price is actually lower than usual. "Sale" labels alone don't count.
  - Fewer, better picks beat endless lists.
  - Be upfront about how we earn money.
- **Won't say:** Fake urgency ("only 2 left!" unless Amazon shows it), "lowest price ever" without proof, "best on Amazon", or paid-review language.

## Audience
### Segment 1 — Everyday Amazon shoppers (primary, broad)
- **Who:** Adults in the US who already shop on Amazon and want to pay less for things they were going to buy anyway.
- **Cares about:** Saving money, not wasting time, not getting burned by junk products.
- **Believes / objects:** "Most 'deals' are fake markdowns." "Influencers just push whatever pays."
- **Where:** Instagram (Reels, carousels, stories), TikTok, Pinterest (search-driven, gift guides).
- **Sophistication:** Beginner to intermediate deal hunters.
### Segment 2 — Seasonal gift shoppers (Oct–Dec, plus Mother's/Father's Day, Valentine's)
- **Who:** People buying for family, partners and friends.
- **Cares about:** Ideas by recipient and budget, gifts that arrive on time.
- **Transformation:** "I have no idea what to get" → "Done, and I saved money."
- **Note:** The audience was chosen as broad. Gift guides are the sharpest hook, so seasonal content leans into segment 2.

## Voice
- **Dimensions:** Formality 2/5 · Warmth 4/5 · Authority 4/5 · Energy 3/5 · Humor 2/5 · Polish 4/5
- **We sound ___, never ___:**
  - helpful, never pushy
  - honest, never hype-y
  - specific, never vague ("machine-washable, 4 colors"), never "amazing!!!"
  - calm and clear, never shouty all-caps
- **Words we use:** "deal", "pick", "marked down", "usual price", "worth it if…", "skip it if…", "check today's price".
- **Words we ban:** "insane deal", "game-changer", "must-have" (unless explaining why), "lowest price ever", "you NEED this", "life-changing", "best on Amazon", "guaranteed".
- **How we handle hard things:** If a price goes back up or a product disappoints, say so plainly and remove or replace the pick.
- **Signature devices:** Open with the problem or the use, not the hype. One or two emojis max per caption, used as markers (🔗 🎁 💡), never strings of them. 5–10 targeted hashtags. "Link in bio" call to action. Always include the disclosure.
- **Voice by platform:** Instagram: helpful mini-reviews. TikTok: same voice, faster, spoken. Pinterest: keyword-rich titles ("Gifts for Her Under $50").
- **Pronunciation guide:** n/a.

## Operational defaults (tool skills read these)
- **On-camera policy:** The owner's real face is the profile photo on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest (same photo everywhere) and may appear in stories (e.g. a quick hello or a reaction). Feed videos (Reels/TikToks) are hands-only with the owner's own voiceover. Never show family members or the home address/exterior.
- **AI & synthetic media policy:** Real hands and the owner's own voice on camera, no AI avatars and no voice clones. AI-generated imagery only for backgrounds or graphics, never to depict a real product; label AI-assisted media where platforms require it.
- **Accessibility defaults:** Alt text on every image (describe the product plainly); captions always on for video; high-contrast text on graphics.
- **Visual identity:** Hot pink & coral palette, Poppins + Inter, tag logo. See `public/styles.css` and `public/img/` (the site is the brand kit). Post Studio in /admin makes on-brand slides.

## Guardrails
- **Banned words/topics:** See "Words we ban"; no political or divisive topics.
- **Compliance / legal (Amazon Associates + FTC):**
  - Every post carries a clear disclosure: `#ad` plus "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." On video, say or show it too.
  - No prices in images or posts that can't be kept current. Say "check today's price" instead.
  - Never use Amazon product photos in social posts; use own photos or video, or emoji/graphics.
  - No affiliate links in emails, PDFs or offline material; link to the site instead.
  - No incentivized clicks ("click my link to enter a giveaway"), no buying through own links.
  - Don't imply Amazon endorses the account; don't use Amazon's logo.
- **Competitor rules:** Don't name other deal accounts.
- **Sensitivity:** Health and supplement products: no health claims.

## Show, don't tell — example posts
- **Loves (style to aim for):**
  - A carousel "5 kitchen gifts that are actually useful" with one line per item on *why*: specific, save-worthy.
  - A hands-only Reel unboxing a deal and showing one honest flaw: trust-building.
  - A "how to tell if an Amazon 'sale' is real" tips carousel: pure value, no push.
- **Hates:**
  - "OMG you NEED this 🔥🔥🔥 link in bio!!!": hype with no reason.
  - Screenshots of prices that are wrong a day later: breaks Amazon rules and trust.
