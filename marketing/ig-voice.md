## My positions

1. Most "sales" aren't real. A crossed-out price that was never charged isn't a discount.
2. Fewer, better picks beat a list of 100 "Amazon finds".
3. Affiliate creators should say plainly how they get paid.
4. The best gift is one they'll use every week, not the flashiest one.
5. Black Friday isn't always the cheapest day. Check the price, don't trust the label.

## Off limits

- **Topics I do not post about:** politics, anything divisive, my family or my home (no family members on camera, no house exterior or address)
- **Clients, numbers or names I cannot say publicly:** none
- **Claims I am not allowed to make:**
  - No prices in images or captions (Amazon Associates rules). Say "check today's price".
  - No Amazon product photos.
  - No "lowest price ever" without proof.
  - No fake urgency or invented stock counts.
  - No health claims for supplements or health products.
  - Never imply Amazon endorses me.
  - Every post carries `#ad` plus "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
