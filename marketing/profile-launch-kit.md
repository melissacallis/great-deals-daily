**Check it's working.** Look at each app's insights every Monday:
- **Instagram:** Professional dashboard → profile visits and link taps.
- **TikTok:** Analytics → profile views.

If there are lots of profile visits but few follows or link taps, the bio needs another pass. In that case, ask me to rework it.
