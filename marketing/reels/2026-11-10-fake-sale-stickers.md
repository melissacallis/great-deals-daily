# Video plan: "Some Black Friday sale stickers are lying to you" (Tue Nov 10)

Based on the 10 attention techniques in the reference video (future pacing, unresolved problems,
situational relatability, context framing, stories, controversy, novelty, insight, 5 senses, common enemy),
applied to the brand's core position: **most "sales" aren't real; I check them so you don't have to.**

- **Length:** 40–45 s, talking to camera (phone, upright, waist up, same spot as the palm video).
- **Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reel (9:16), Pinterest (2:3, "Tap to shop").
- **Effects we already have:** palm cards with % bursts, couch wall, digital zoom, captions.
  **New for this video (I build them before editing):** *chart grow* (price-history line) and *checklist*.
- **The common enemy:** fake "List Price" markdowns (the sticker), never a brand or seller by name.

## Script and beat sheet

| Time | You say | On screen / effect | Technique |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Black Friday is 17 days away, and some of these sale stickers are lying to you." | Punch-in zoom on your face; a big "-70%" sticker slaps on the wall behind you, then a red ✗ stamps it | Common enemy + controversy |
| 3–5 s | "Stay to the end: I'll show you the one deal I'd actually buy today." | Small "the one deal ↓" tag at the bottom | Future pacing |
| 5–10 s | "You see 60% off, you add it to cart… and next week it's cheaper. Been there?" | Quick couch-wall pins: cart, "60% OFF" sticker, then "now cheaper 😩" | Situational relatability |
| 10–17 s | "Here's what nobody tells you: that crossed-out 'List Price' is often a price nobody ever paid." | Zoom into a card's crossed-out "List Price", circled | Insight + unresolved problem |
| 17–27 s | "A real deal looks like this: the price drops below what it usually sells for. A fake one looks like this: the 'sale' price IS the usual price." | **Chart grow** behind you: line drops (green ✓ + ding), then a flat line (red ✗ + buzz) | Context framing + 5 senses |
| 27–35 s | "So before I post anything, I check three things." (hold hand up, count on fingers) | **Checklist** pops beside you: ✓ beats its usual price, ✓ not a fake list price, ✓ good reviews | Novelty + insight |
| 35–41 s | "Like this one: it passed all three." (palm out) | Today's best checked deal lands on your palm with its % OFF burst | Story + payoff (closes the loop from 0:03) |
| 41–45 s | "I only post deals that beat their usual price. All 50 are checked and in my bio; prices change daily." | "LINK IN BIO" sticker; on Pinterest "TAP TO SHOP" | Common enemy (what you stand for) + CTA |

Say it in your own words: calm, like a friend who did the homework. Avoid "insane", "must-have",
"lowest price ever" (unless you've proven it), and don't call any specific seller fake.

## Why this should hold attention

- **Opens a loop in the first 5 seconds** ("the one deal I'd buy") and closes it at 35 s, so people stay.
- **Picks a side** (fake sale stickers vs. real deals), which makes it shareable without naming anyone.
- **Teaches something people can use** (the 3 checks), so it gets saved and shared, which helps reach.
- **Changes something on screen every 3–5 seconds** (zoom, pins, chart, checklist, palm card).
