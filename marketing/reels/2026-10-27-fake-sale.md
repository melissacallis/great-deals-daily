```
Nobody tells you Amazon's crossed-out price isn't always real.
So 60% off? Could be the normal price.
Sign one: it says "List Price."
That's often retail, not what it sold for.
Sign two. Flat price history.
I paste the link into Keepa.
Same price for 3 months? I skip it.
Sign three: the whole brand is 50% off.
All year.
A real deal beats its usual price, and on Amazon that's the only thing I check.
Save this for Black Friday, and check that crossed-out price.
```
