# Black Friday 2026 Campaign: Great Deals Daily (@datadarling33 · @datadarling)

**Dates:** Mon Oct 26 – Sun Dec 13, 2026 · **Black Friday:** Fri Nov 27 · **Cyber Monday:** Mon Nov 30

## The one-page brief

- **One goal:** reach **10 qualifying Amazon sales within a 30-day window**. That unlocks the Creators API (live Top 50 deals) and proves the model.
- **How we measure (real numbers only):**
  - Amazon Associates Central → Reports → *Ordered items*, checked every Monday.
  - Instagram / TikTok Insights → link-in-bio taps and profile visits.
  - Pinterest Analytics → outbound clicks.
- **Story in one line:** *"Skip the fake sales. We check the deals so you don't have to."* This runs through all three phases.
- **One call to action, everywhere:** "Tap the link in bio" → `great-deals-daily-production.up.railway.app/swipe`
- **Platforms:** Instagram (main), TikTok, Pinterest.
- **Realistic weekly load:**
  - Instagram: 4 feed posts, plus stories most days
  - TikTok: 3 videos
  - Pinterest: 5 pins
  - About 4–5 hours a week, batched on Sunday.
- **Rules on every post (from the brand profile):**
  - `#ad` plus "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." (say it out loud in videos too)
  - No prices in images or captions; say "check today's price".
  - No Amazon product photos; use your own photos or video, or Post Studio emoji slides.
  - **Credible urgency only:** "deal prices can end anytime". No fake countdowns, no invented "only 3 left".
  - At least 1 in 4 posts is pure value (no selling).
