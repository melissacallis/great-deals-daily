Privacy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: UBottega has no accounts, no sign-ups and no email list. We don’t sell your data, and there are no ad networks on the site . With your OK, we use the Pinterest tag to see which pins bring visitors. Say no and it never loads.

UBottega (www.ubottega.com) is run by Data Darling. It lists Amazon deals and links to them on Amazon. This page explains what happens with your information when you visit.

What we collect

  • Basic server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider (Railway) records each request to the site: your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time. We use this only to keep the site running and secure (for example, spotting bots and errors). We don’t use it to build profiles of visitors.
  • Deal taps. When you tap a deal, the link passes through our site on its way to Amazon so we can count which deals people like. We record the product, the page you tapped it on, the time and whether it was a phone. Only if you accepted cookies, we also record which app or site you came from (for example Pinterest or TikTok). This tap record does not include your IP address, name or email.
  • Pinterest tag (only if you accept). If you click “Accept”, we load the Pinterest tag, which sets a Pinterest cookie and tells Pinterest that you visited and tapped deals, so we can see which pins work. Pinterest handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you click “Decline”, or haven’t chosen yet, it doesn’t load.

Amazon

Product photos on this site are loaded from Amazon’s servers. When you tap a deal you leave UBottega for Amazon, and Amazon’s own privacy notice applies there. Amazon uses its own cookies to credit us for purchases.

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you buy through a link here, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent personal data, and we don’t share it for targeted advertising beyond the optional Pinterest tag above.
  • We don’t collect names, emails, phone numbers or payment details. Purchases happen on Amazon, not here.
  • The site isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Cookie choice: , or with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as “Decline” and don’t load the Pinterest tag unless you choose “Accept” yourself.
  • Texas and other US residents can ask what we hold about them, ask us to delete it, or opt out of targeted advertising. Since we don’t keep records tied to a name or email, there is usually nothing to look up, but you can ask anytime.
